## Script ↔ Background match (sabse badi seekh)
- Mukku: bg SYSTEM constant (texture+doodles+pools), card content + sticker bilkul bole gaye
  shabd par badalta hai. Background "sajaawat" hai, kahani card me hai.
- MK: bg KHUD kahani hai — har line ke saath mahaul/prop script se match karke badalta hai.
- Dono me bg me TEXTURE pakka hai: dot grid / graph grid / doodles / light pools / vignette.
  Plain flat colour KAHIN nahi mila.

## Hamara naya BG system (Mint Sticker par lagu)
Layer 1 (pehchan, constant): mint base + halka dot-grid + bikhre doodles (gola, taara, squiggle)
  + soft light pools — kabhi plain colour nahi.
Layer 2 (script-matched, har scene): scene ki theme ki sketch sajawat card ke aas-paas —
  captions reel: S1 phone ke gird notes, S2 behte sound-waves (lag chuke hain), S3 upload teer,
  S4 timeline sketch; har nayi reel me scene ke hisaab se doodle-set badlega.
Layer 3 (per-word prop): bubble ke paas bole gaye shabd par chhota sticker (paisa, taala,
  ghadi...) pop + float — mukku wala tareeka, hamare mint rangon me.

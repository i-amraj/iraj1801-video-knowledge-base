## Script ke steps — Ricks wali 7-Beat Recipe (pakki)
Source: `~/workspace/your_files/story_recipe_7beat.md` (Ricks kit STORY_TO_FILM.md)
1. **Weird hook** — 1 line, <15 shabd, ajeeb baat bina context
2. **Stakes** — 2–3 lines, baat badi kyun hai, ek NUMBER ke saath
3. **Clue** — 1–2 lines, hero ko dikhi wo cheez jo baakiyon ko nahi dikhi
4. **Mockery** — 2–3 lines, sab hero par hanste/bewakoof samajhte hain
5. **Payoff** — 1–2 lines, saboot ki hero sahi tha
6. **Science reveal** — 1–2 lines, asli wajah aasan shabdon me
7. **Arguable last line** — 1 line, comment me behes wali

Niyam: kul **150–180 shabd, 14–20 lines**; har line = 1 shot; har line ke 1–3 khaas shabd *stars* me (caption me gold); facts 2+ sources se verify, invent kabhi nahi; script ke upar **SHARE TRIGGER + COMMENT TRIGGER** naam se declare.

### Tool-reel par is recipe ka istemal (hamari mapping)
- Weird hook = tool ka sabse ajeeb RESULT pehle frame me ("Ek line likhi… poori reel ban gayi")
- Stakes = mehenga/paid tareeka kitna kharcha + samay khata hai (number ke saath)
- Clue = free tool ki ek khaas khubi jo paid me bhi nahi
- Mockery = "log kehte hain free wala bakwaas hoga" / paid wala dost hansta hai
- Payoff = free tool ka asal result screen par (saboot)
- Science reveal = tool andar se kaise kaam karta hai, aasan shabdon me
- Last line = behes + comment keyword ("PAID ya FREE? Comment me keyword likho, link follow ke baad milega")

# DIRECTOR STORYBOARD FRAMEWORK — 8 STEPS (Raj, 2026-11-15)
Har video se pehle ye 8 steps lazmi hain. Kuch bhi skip nahi. Pehle ye poora hoga,
phir Raj ka approval gate, uske baad hi production / final video prompts.

- **STEP 1 – Characters:** har character: kaun hai, umar, shakal, kapde, body type,
  personality, mood/emotion, har scene me behaviour. Haath, chehra, kapde har scene me
  CONSISTENT.
- **STEP 2 – Scene breakdown:** har scene: location, time, weather, wahan kya-kya ho sakta hai.
- **STEP 3 – Objects & environment:** har cheez: jinda (move/reaction/speed kya) ya nirjeev
  saman (kahan rakha hai, physics — gravity/hawa/takraav — ka asar hoga ya nahi).
- **STEP 4 – Camera & visuals:** har shot: angle (low/high/close-up/wide), movement
  (pan/zoom/tracking), lighting, colour tone, overall style.
- **STEP 5 – Motion & continuity:** har character/object ki movement step-by-step;
  scene-to-scene kapde/props/lighting/position match check; choti galtiyan (extra fingers,
  galat haath, gayab objects) PEHLE pakdo.
- **STEP 6 – Sound & timing:** har scene ki duration, BGM, SFX, dialogue, voice tone.
- **STEP 7 – Final storyboard:** scene-by-scene TABLE (Scene no. | Description | Characters |
  Camera | Movement | Sound | Duration).
- **STEP 8 – Review:** galtiyan/logic problems ki list + fixes. Uske baad hi final video prompts.

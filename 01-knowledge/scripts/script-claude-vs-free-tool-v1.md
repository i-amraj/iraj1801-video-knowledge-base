## Script (har line = 1 shot; *stars* = caption me gold shabd)

**Beat 1 — Weird Hook**
1. Ek *line* likhi… aur poori *reel* ban gayi।

**Beat 2 — Stakes**
2. Main *Claude Pro* lene wala tha — *do hazaar* rupaye mahina।
3. Socha tha, paise dunga to *video* khud ban jayegi।
4. Par pehla *sach* koi batata hi nahi।

**Beat 3 — Clue**
5. Claude *video* nahi banata — wo sirf *code* deta hai।
6. Us code ko chalana, *render* karna — sab *tumhare* sar par।

**Beat 4 — Mockery**
7. Mera dost bola — "*Pro* wale bhaiya, ab *editor* bhi ban jao!" aur has pada।
8. Sach ye hai, aadhi janta aise hi *paise* phansati hai।

**Beat 5 — Payoff**
9. Phir mujhe ek *free tool* mila — naam *comment* me milega।
10. Hindi me ek line likhi — "*meri agency ki reel bana do*" — aur reel *taiyaar*।
11. Script, *awaaz*, visuals — teeno usi ne jode। *Zero* rupaye।

**Beat 6 — Science Reveal**
12. Andar ye hota hai — tool tumhari line ko *scenes* me todta hai।
13. Har scene par *awaaz* aur *tasveer* khud bitha deta hai।
14. Ek *sach* aur — free plan me chhota sa *watermark* aata hai; *try* karna bilkul free hai।

**Beat 7 — Arguable Last Line**
15. Ab tum faisla karo — *do hazaar* wala Pro, ya *free* wala dimaag? Comment me *VIDEO* likho, tool follow karke milega।

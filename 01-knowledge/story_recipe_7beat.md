# STORY RECIPE — 7-Beat "Laughed-At Genius" (Raj lock, 2026-11-16)

Source: Ricks Clips kit STORY_TO_FILM.md — Raj ka order: "Ab hum apni next video ko ek story ki trh dikhayenge… kuch ye style hona chaiye script ka."

## Kahani chunne ka formula
**Weird Practice + Logon ki Hansi + Chhupa Science** — ek ajeeb cheez jo ek frame me dikhe, jise dekh kar koi pehle hanse, aur jiske peeche ek aasan si asli wajah ho.

## 7 beats
1. **Weird hook** (1 line, <15 shabd) — ajeeb action, bina context
2. **Stakes** (2–3 lines) — baat badi kyun hai, ek number ke saath
3. **Clue** (1–2 lines) — hero ko dikhi wo ajeeb cheez jo baakiyon ko nahi dikhi
4. **Mockery** (2–3 lines) — sab hero par hanste hain, bewakoof samajhte hain
5. **Payoff** (1–2 lines) — saboot ki hero sahi tha
6. **Science reveal** (1–2 lines) — asli wajah, aasan shabdon me
7. **Arguable last line** (1 line) — comment me behes wali

## Niyam
- Kul **150–180 shabd, 14–20 lines**; har line = ek shot (2–4s)
- Ek line me sirf ek baat; chhote sentences
- Har line ke **1–3 khaas shabd *stars* me** — caption me wahi gold chamkenge
- Facts pehle **2+ sources se verify**; pakka na ho to naram shabd ("kaha jata hai", "about"); quotes/ghatnayein invent KABHI nahi
- Drama rakho, facts ke saath imaandaari se

## Hamare lane ke locks ke saath sangam
- Beat 7 = hamara **COMMENT TRIGGER** (DO-NAZARIYA: science ya aastha — behes wahi)
- Legend hamesha "kehte hain/maanyata" ke roop me; tathya bankar kabhi nahi
- Star-shabd = hamare phrase-caption ka gold keyword
- Script ke upar SHARE TRIGGER + COMMENT TRIGGER naam se declare honge (standing rule)
- Character: simple gol-face 2D (Raj, 2026-11-16); backgrounds real 3D

## Emotions (cutouts2/ + poses me copy)
neutral, grin, laugh, surprised, shocked, thinking, wink, talking, determined

## Acting poses (poses/ + cutouts2/)
- listening — kaan par haath, sunne ka pose ("awaaz sunke", "suno")
- pointing — ungli upar, step/idea announce ("Step ek", "ek tareeka hai")
- thumbsup — angutha upar, payoff/approval ("ho gaya", "bilkul free")
- phone — haath me phone ("video upload karo", "phone par dekho")
- explaining — khuli hatheli, samjhane ka gesture (general bayan)
- waving — haath hila kar hello (intro/outro)
